Market Context

Before talking about the product, consider a fact: more and more people in Vietnam are choosing self-organized group trips — and more of them are stuck scrolling through dozens of Zalo messages just to figure out who owes whom.

Context

Vietnam's tourism industry is entering a period of strong growth.

Self-organized group travel is becoming a trend.

Young people are the most active and proactive travelers.

The 18–24 age group has the highest rate of self-planning, reflecting a preference for organizing and owning their travel experience.

02

User Interview

Three people, three different roles in a travel group — but one thing in common: everyone is tired, each for a different reason.

Đỗ Thị Bích Liên

35 y/o · Chief Accountant
Leader · The Burdened Organizer
Habits

Plans itineraries and tracks expenses manually on Google Sheets; uses Zalo as the main channel to update the group.

Pain Points
  • Time-consuming to type each itinerary and expense line into Sheets on a small phone screen.
  • Every update floods the Zalo group, burying important messages.
  • Pressure to collect paper receipts, check bank history, and get the math 100% right at the end of the trip.
Expectations

An "all-in-one" app that automates data entry, splitting, and group updates — reducing the need for manual Zalo notifications.

"I need one place that handles everything automatically, so I don't have to message on Zalo or calculate every receipt by hand."

Nguyễn Minh

27 y/o · Marketing Staff
Tech-savvy Leader
Habits

Researches multiple sources when planning; budgets in Excel and uses Splitwise to manage debts during the trip.

Pain Points
  • Tool fragmentation: switching between Excel, Zalo/Messenger, and Splitwise.
  • Disconnected data: expenses in Splitwise don't map back to activities in Excel.
  • Time lost entering small expenses on the go; at the end, must reconcile two platforms and chase each person for payment.
Expectations

All trip info in one place. Log expenses quickly, and let the system auto-remind payments after the trip.

"I want to stop jumping between Excel, chat, and Splitwise for just one trip."

Lê Hạ Lan

21 y/o · University Student
Passive Follower · Dependent Member
Habits

Passively receives info via Zalo/Messenger; transfers a deposit whenever the organizer asks, rarely checks the itinerary or budget herself.

Pain Points
  • Loses track of information: doesn't know how the group is spending or what tool is being used.
  • Lack of transparency: at the end, only gets a final total, with no way to see what it was actually spent on.
Expectations

A shared app to follow the whole itinerary, know her balance in real time, and pay instantly instead of waiting to be reminded.

"I just want to see clearly what I owe and for what, and pay it right away — without waiting for anyone to remind me."

03

User Persona

From those three stories, a common persona emerges — the one person almost every friend group has.

Nguyễn Minh Anh

Group Trip Organizer · 28 y/o · Office Worker

🎯 Goals

  • Manage itinerary, expenses, and debts in one single app.
  • Track spending transparently by activity and by member.
  • Automate the expense summary, splitting, and payment reminders after the trip.

😤 Frustrations

  • Has to use multiple tools: Google Sheets, Zalo/Messenger, and a splitting app.
  • Time lost on manual entry, reconciling expenses, and updates.
  • Members struggle to follow the itinerary and don't know what money went where.

📱 Behaviors

  • Usually organizes group trips of 4–8 people.
  • Mainly uses a phone, combined with Google Sheets, Zalo, and an expense app.
  • Habitually checks the budget and updates spending throughout the trip.

🧭 Context

  • Takes on the responsibility of planning for the whole group.
  • Wants to reduce manual work and repetitive notifications.
"I want all the info about the itinerary, expenses, and payments in one place — instead of managing it across a bunch of different apps."

05

Problem Statement

  • Managing a self-organized group trip still relies on multiple tools and manual work.
  • Itinerary, members, and expenses are managed across different platforms, causing poor sync, hard tracking, and frequent errors.
  • Summarizing costs, settling up, and tracking debts mostly falls on the organizer, increasing their coordination load and lowering overall trip-management efficiency.
06

Problem–Solution Fit

Solution Concept

  • TravelLoop is an all-in-one mobile app that lets self-organized travel groups manage the entire trip on one platform.
  • Integrates: planning, building the itinerary, group collaboration, expense management, and post-trip payments.
  • Optimizes coordination among members, increases transparency, and delivers a seamless travel experience.
